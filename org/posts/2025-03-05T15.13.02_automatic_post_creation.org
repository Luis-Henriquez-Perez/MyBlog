To create a new post I can also just rely on a function.

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(defun oo-new-blog-post ()
  "Create a new blog post, prompting for the name."
  (interactive)
  (let* (blog-dir name)
    (setq blog-dir "~/Documents/MyBlog/org/posts/")
    ;; Prompt of the name.
    (setq name (read-string "Name: "))
    ;; Prepend the timestamp to the name.
    (find-file (expand-file-name name blog-dir))))
#+end_src
