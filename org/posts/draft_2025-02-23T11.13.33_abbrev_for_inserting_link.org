An abbrev that will prompt for a buffer and convert it to html.  The buffer could be a live buffer or a so-called "virtual" buffer.

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(defun! oo-expand-blog-buffer ()
  "Prompt for a buffer, convert it into html and save it as a link."
  (htmlize-buffer (get-buffer))
  )
#+end_src
