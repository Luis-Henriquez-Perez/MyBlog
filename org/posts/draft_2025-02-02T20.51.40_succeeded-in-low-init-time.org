I have succeeded in making my emacs pretty near to as fast as possible--the init
time is about 1.4 seconds, most of it being due to [[][Fpackage-initialize]] and
setting the font.  My whole process convinced me that it is crazy hard to make
emacs that fast.  Well to be honest, I had (and still have) a lot of junk that
have had to refactor and clean up to get my emacs configuration to such a low
time.  Also I had not compiled my config and because the macros I use in my
configuration are so prevalent in my code, my configuration is one of the few
that benefits significantly from byte-compilation.
