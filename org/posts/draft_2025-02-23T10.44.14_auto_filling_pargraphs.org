#+author:Luis Henriquez-Perez

For far too long I have wanted Emacs to automatically fill my paragraphs like
all the other non-Emacs text editors I have used previously.  I looked at an
external package.

There is also a relevant issue [[][]].  It recommends [[file:/home/luis/Documents/MyBlog/org/documentation/29.4-helpful-callable--refill-mode.html][refill-mode]], which is the
closest I could find for this.  The problem with this mode is that it joins
adjacent paragraphs separated by newlines which I do not want.

I did not find anything at least on a precursory search.

This is something that needs a minor mode that I can toggle on and off.  But the
main part of the body is simply going to be adding a local hook to
[[file:/home/luis/Documents/MyBlog/org/documentation/29.4-helpful-variable--post-command-hook.html][post-command-hook]] that calls [[file:/home/luis/Documents/MyBlog/org/documentation/29.4-helpful-callable--fill-paragraph.html][fill-paragraph]].

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(defun oo--post-command-hook--fill-paragraph-h (&rest _)
  (call-interactively #'fill-paragraph))

(defun! oo-toggle-auto-fill-paragraph ()
  (interactive)
  (set! hook #'oo--post-command-hook--fill-paragraph-h)
  (if (member hook post-self-insert-hook)
      (remove-hook 'post-self-insert-hook hook 'local)
    (add-hook 'post-self-insert-hook hook nil 'local)))
#+end_src
