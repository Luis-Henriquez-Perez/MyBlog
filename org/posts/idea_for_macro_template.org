#+TITLE: idea for macro template
#+AUTHOR: Luis Henriquez-Perez

I was switching back and forth between =skeleton= and =tempel= and then while I was showering it occurred to me how nice it would be if a template was implemented in terms of a macro.

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(defmacro deftemplate! (name &rest body)
  (declare (indent defun))
  )

(deftemplate! foo
  "(defun ")

;; This would expand to

(progn (insert "(defun "))

(deftemplate! foo
  "(defun " p " ()")

(progn (insert "(defun ")
       (setmark)
       (insert "()"))
#+end_src
