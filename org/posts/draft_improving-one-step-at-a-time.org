#+title: Little by Little
#+author: Luis Henriquez-Perez

I have been notoriously bad at gradual improvement over several days.  This is
one of the most important life skills.  But I feel that most people are not good
at.  And particularly in programming this is important.  You want to change your
goals form DO THIS to position my self so I can do this.  You just want to get
closer and closer.
