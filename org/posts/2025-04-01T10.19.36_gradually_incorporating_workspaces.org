#+title:gradually incorporating workspaces
#+author:Luis Henriquez-Perez

I have wanted workspaces for a long time.  There are a plethora of workspace
packages in Emacs: [[https://github.com/nex3/perspective-el][perspective]], [[https://github.com/pashinin/workgroups2][workgroups]], [[https://depp.brause.cc/eyebrowse/][eyebrowse]] and even the built-in
[[][desktop.el]] just to name a few.  These session managers each have a way of
saving buffer information so that you can restore your current Emacs window
configuration and all the buffers therein.  Usually they have their own
"serialization".  What makes burly special is instead of adding support for
several things (re)uses Emacs bookmark support.  Which is quite elegant and
clever because it does not reinvent the wheel.  I wanted to use [[https://github.com/alphapapa/burly.el][burly]] for
workspaces.

This is a project so I want to take it helo a step at time
