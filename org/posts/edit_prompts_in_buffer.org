#+title:edit prompts in buffer
#+author:Luis Henriquez-Perez

One thing I have always wanted to do is edit prompts in Emacs buffers.  Several
Emacs functions prompt for a regular expression and when they prompt me I have
always hated having to come up with one on the fly.  First, I strongly prefer
writing regular expression in the [[][rx]] format, but none of the functions
that prompt for a regexp accept that format.  But also I have wanted to be able
to craft and edit what I enter into a prompt just like I edit text in every
other buffer.  Instead of a one line prompt I would like a buffer to come up
where I can enter the information at my leisure and with full editing
capabilities and only then when I am ready invoke =C-c= or some other keybinding to
actually pass in the contents of the buffer to whatever function prompted me for
it.

So as always the first step is to research the problem for existing solutions.
