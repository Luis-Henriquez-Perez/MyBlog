Recently I saw a video on youtube where this developer has decided to switch
from emacs to vim and he explains the reasons why.  To be honest I do not
understand the first reason.

But I do get the second reason he gave: speed.  I feel that vim is so fast that
as long as you do not do anything stupid or crazy when writing your vimrc, you
should not really have to think about performance and you will have virtually no
latency as you type and your startup time will be fast.  That is not the case
with emacs.  It is /extremely/ easy to have 5+ seconds startup time even when
trying to follow lazy-loading principles.  And it is extremely easy to end up
with latency as you type.  I was shocked when in the video the developer pointed
out he had a 5 second startup time /and/ was using doom emacs.  I thought that
doom emacs users had at most a 1 second startup time.  I found that revelation
quite disheartening.  Considering how hard doom
tries to be fast.
