#+title:escaping org emphasis markers
#+author:Luis Henriquez-Perez

When I was trying to write my previous blog post I realized that I did not know
how to escape an asterix emphasis marker.  I looked up how to do it online.

One answer recommended the use of a [[][]] zero-width space.

[[https://emacs.stackexchange.com/questions/16688/how-can-i-escape-the-in-org-mode-to-prevent-bold-fontification][]]

Another said that a better solution is to use [[][org-entities]].  I had no idea
what =org-entities= where.  Apparently they are a way of displaying special
characters in org-mode.

[[][]]

I want to use the entities to censor curse words.  Honestly it is probably
better just not to use curse words at all but when I thought about the idea of
having another potential use of abbrevs I got excited. 

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(define-abbrev global-abbrev-table "fuck" "\\ast\\ast\\ast\\ast" nil :enable-function #'oo-in-org-mode-p)
(define-abbrev global-abbrev-table "shit" "\\ast\\ast\\ast\\ast" nil :enable-function #'oo-in-org-mode-p)
#+end_src
