#+title:escaping org emphasis markers
#+author:Luis Henriquez-Perez

When I was trying to write my previous blog post I ended up cursing in my
writing.  I was considering whether to curse mildly in my blog posts.  And it
was then that I had the idea to use abbrevs to censor any curses I wrote by
replacing the text.  I was excited because censoring words is a completely new
way of using abbrevs that I had never thought of.  When I actually set off
creating abbrevs I realized that I did not know how to escape an asterisk
emphasis marker.

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(define-abbrev global-abbrev-table "fuck" "****" nil :enable-function #'oo-use-text-abbrev-p)
(define-abbrev global-abbrev-table "shit" "****" nil :enable-function #'oo-use-text-abbrev-p)
#+end_src

I looked up how to do it online.  But in doing this I noticed
org was interpreting the asterisks as emphasis markers as opposed to literally.
I tried to add a backslash (=\=) just as I would in a regular expression and that
did not work either.  Well, it did display the asterisk literally but it also
displayed the backslash.

I looked for answers in a promising [[https://emacs.stackexchange.com/questions/16688/how-can-i-escape-the-in-org-mode-to-prevent-bold-fontification][emacs stackexchange question]].

One answer recommended the use of a zero-width space.  But I soon discarded the
idea when I read the answer below that claimed to be a better solution.
[[][org-entities]].  I had no idea what =org-entities= where before reading this
post.  Apparently they are a way of displaying special characters in org-mode.

I want to use the entities to censor curse words.  Honestly it is probably
better just not to use curse words at all but when I thought about the idea of
having another potential use of abbrevs I got excited. 

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(define-abbrev global-abbrev-table "fuck" "\\ast\\ast\\ast\\ast" nil :enable-function #'oo-in-org-mode-p)
(define-abbrev global-abbrev-table "shit" "\\ast\\ast\\ast\\ast" nil :enable-function #'oo-in-org-mode-p)
#+end_src

Thinking about this a bit longer I do not think I am going to censor curse words
after all or at least not all of them.  Instead I think it might be better to
just tone them down.  So "fuck" would become "darn it".  I am not sure.  I am
leaning towards "fuck" to become "blast" or "curses".
