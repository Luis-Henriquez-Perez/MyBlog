Welcome! This is the repo for my personal blog.

See at [[https://luishp.blog/]].
# * I want this blog to be...
# - *informative*
#   Every post should be information rich.
# - *thorough*
#   I should thoroughly explore topics.  I do not want this superficial stuff.
# - *extensively documented*
#   Everything that I am referecing should have a link to what I am referring to.
#   In general each post will be link heavy.
# - *thoughtful*
#   The thoughts and descriptions I add should be high-quality.  It should be
#   clear from reading as well as my references that the thoughts are
#   well-informed.
# - *entertaining*
#   I am not going to try to change how write to try to be entertaining, but I do
#   hope that my posts are as entertaining to others as they are to me.
# - *honest and soulful*
#   Part of the reason I wrote the blog as to use as a resume for employers to
#   actually see my work.  But I am not trying to impress them and I am not going
#   to.  I want this blog to be unapologetic and frank.
# - *visually appealing but minimal*
#   Right now I have the minimal part down but am severly lacking in the "visually
#   applealing" department because if my lack of experience with css.  But I will
#   get there.  And I will be documenting exactly how I get there in this blog.
# - *close to the metal* The website should be in hand-coded html, css and
#   javascript.  No [[https://wordpress.com/][wordpress]] and no [[][org-mode]].  Maybe I will introduce
#   common-lisp in the form of [[https://github.com/ruricolist/spinneret][snipperet]] or even [[][]] in the future as I
#   become more familiar with html because I do think it is a lot of boilerplate
#   and I would like to automate it with lisp macros.
# - *authentic* My site is *not* AI generated nor are the contents of my blog posts.
#   It is written and planned be me, a human being.
#   At most I might use AI to research possible answer for a problem /and then
#   confirm whether the answer is true or false/ before I use it.  I have
#   encountered /several/ cases where answers generated by AI have been wrong.
# * I am writing this blog to...
# - *document problems*
#   Although there is a plethora of support in terms of stackoverflow questions
#   blogs and articles about text and even the archwiki.  Often I found problems
#   difficult to explain.
# - *help others*
#   I want others not to have to struggle as I have had with the problems I have
#   encountered.  I have often had the experience where I have scowered the web
#   for trying to figure out a difficult problem.
# - *help myself*
#   I have moments when I forget exactly what I did or how I did it and in those
#   moments I would like an account of how I did something.
# - *document how to teach yourself*
#   I want to show the process of how I think about problems and how I go about
#   devising solutions to them and learning about them.  And how I go about
#   figuring them out without a teacher or mentor or boss telling me what to do.
# - *be a resume*
#   I want this to serve a repository for my work.  In my past I have been
#   notoriously bad at saving my work and reusing it and really advertising
#   myself.
