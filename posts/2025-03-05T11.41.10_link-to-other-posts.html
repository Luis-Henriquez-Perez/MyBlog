link to other posts

Similar in spirit to a previous post, I want to showcase creating an abbrev which would expand into a link to another blog post. 

(defun oo-expand-blog-post-link ()
  "Prompt for another blog post and insert link to that post."
  (let (post-dir current-post posts chosen description)
    ;; Ensure we are in the blog directory.
    (setq post-dir "~/Documents/MyBlog/org/posts/")
    (setq current-post (expand-file-name (buffer-file-name)))
    (cl-assert (equal (expand-file-name post-dir) (file-name-directory current-post)) nil)
    ;; Find the other posts, do not include drafts and of course do not include
    (setq posts (remove (file-name-nondirectory current-post)
                        (directory-files post-dir nil date2name-datetime-regexp)))
    ;; Select one of these posts.
    (unwind-protect (setq chosen (completing-read "Post: " posts))
      ;; If no blog post is chosen just abort and try to put things back where
      ;; we found them.  This is a work in progress, right now I am trying to
      ;; work out how this should work.
      (unless chosen
        (unexpand-abbrev)
        (forward-word)))
    ;; Enter the text of what you want the link to say.  By default it should be
    ;; the title of the post.
    ;; Get tittle of the post
    (setq description (read-string "link description: " nil nil "foo"))
    ;; Make a link to it relative to the current-post directory.
    (insert (format "[[file:%s][%s]]" chosen description)))
  ;; Make sure to return non-nil so that no space insertion works.
  t
  )

Now to make the abbrev. I do not set it to plk because that is what I am using for a link idea in another post. 

(define-abbrev global-abbrev-table "polk" "" 'oo-expand-blog-post-link :enable-function #'oo-in-blog-post-p)

And voilà, now whenever I type "plk" I seamlessly insert a link to another blog post.
